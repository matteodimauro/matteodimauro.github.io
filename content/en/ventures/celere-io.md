**celere.io** is a platform to organize real queues with a simple, fast, and trackable system.

It reduces waiting times and helps coordinate access, check-ins, and customer service in real-time via QR and app.

## Key Features

- **Reduced waiting times:** Eliminates messy queues and shows real-time status to customers and operators.
- **Clear operational flow:** Dashboard, position management, history, and notifications to control every queue with precision.
- **Instant onboarding:** Users join via QR in seconds: app if installed, web guest if not available.

## How It Works

1. The manager creates the queue and generates a unique QR code.
2. The participant scans and receives an anonymous identifier.
3. Position, status, and times are updated in real-time.
4. If desired, the participant can transfer their position from the web area to the app.
